How to read this

Returns are assumed fully refunded, with resaleable products. “Loss per return” should include unrecovered shipping, fees, handling and damage. Ads % here means ad spend divided by gross placed-order value, not ad-attributed ROAS. Enter other selling fees per completed order, such as an allocation of storage and optional programme costs. The monthly result is an estimate, not a Seller Central settlement or a complete financial statement. Taxes on Amazon fees assume eligible input credit. Validate your own tax position and reconcile with actual settlements.

For a quick decision: sort by contribution, review the loss-making SKUs, then test how a higher ad cost or return rate changes your strongest products. Compare products on the same time period and cost basis.
